## How to sign

When you open your first pull request, a bot asks you to sign. Reply in the pull request with this exact comment:

> I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA

Your GitHub username, the date and the pull request are recorded in this repository. If you contribute on behalf of your employer, make sure you are allowed to sign for it.

Why a CLA at all? Without it, every contributor would keep the sole right to license their part of the code, and the project couldn't change or add licensing terms, for example to offer a commercial license to companies that can't use AGPL software, without tracking down every one of them. Whatever happens, every version released under the AGPL stays available under it.
